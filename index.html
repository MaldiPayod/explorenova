Barangay Novaliches

Barangay Novaliches is a residential and commercial community located in Quezon City, Metro Manila. It serves as a center for various local activities, businesses, and public services that cater to the needs of its residents. The barangay contributes to the development of the surrounding community through local governance, infrastructure, and community-based programs. Its accessibility to commercial establishments and essential services makes it an important part of the Novaliches area.
